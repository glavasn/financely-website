Questions

The things people ask us most.

How much deposit do I need?

Many lenders want 20% to avoid Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI). Under the Australian Government's Home Guarantee Scheme, eligible first home buyers can buy with a 5% deposit, and eligible single parents with 2%, without paying LMI. We'll check what you qualify for.

What does it cost to use financely?

For most home loans, nothing. The lender pays us a commission when your loan settles, and it doesn't change your interest rate. If a fee ever applies to your situation, we'll tell you in writing before you go ahead.

Will talking to you affect my credit score?

No. An initial chat doesn't involve a credit check. We only run one when you're ready to apply, and we'll tell you before we do.

What is a comparison rate?

It combines the interest rate with most of a loan's fees and charges, so loans are easier to compare. It's calculated on a standard example of $150,000 over 25 years, so we'll show you what it means for your actual loan amount.

Can you help if I'm self-employed?

Yes. Lenders treat business income very differently. Some need two years of tax returns, some one, and some accept BAS statements. We match you to a lender whose policy fits how you earn.
